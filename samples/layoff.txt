Team,

Today I'm sharing some difficult news. After a thorough review of our priorities, we have made the hard decision to reduce our workforce by approximately 12%, impacting around 340 roles across Product, Sales, and Operations.

This was not a decision we made lightly. The macroeconomic headwinds we discussed last quarter have persisted, and we need to build a leaner, more focused Corvane that can win in this next chapter.

Impacted colleagues will receive an email from People Operations within the next hour. Everyone affected will receive a separation package, extended healthcare, and career transition support.

To those leaving us: thank you. Your contributions shaped who we are, and I am deeply grateful for everything you have given to this journey.

For those staying, I know this is hard. We will come out of this stronger, more agile, and better positioned for long-term growth. I'll share more at Thursday's all-hands.

Pat Ellery
CEO, Corvane Systems
