Hi everyone,

Over the past few years we have learned a lot about how we work best. While remote work gave us flexibility, we have also seen what we lose when we are apart: the spontaneous collaboration, the mentorship, and the energy that makes Halverly special.

Starting March 2, we are asking all employees to work from our offices four days a week. Teams will coordinate their in-office days with their managers, and badge data will help us understand how our spaces are being used.

We know this is a change, and we want to support you through it. We are upgrading our hubs with new collaboration spaces and free lunch on Tuesdays and Thursdays.

Exceptions will be rare and must be approved by your VP. I'm excited to see more of you in person and to rebuild the culture that got us here.

Jordan Vale
Chief People Officer, Halverly
