How it works

Everything runs on your device. Instant mode uses a built-in phrasebook and pattern rules. AI mode downloads a small open model (Qwen2.5 0.5B Instruct, Apache 2.0) from Hugging Face once, keeps it in your browser cache, and runs it locally. There are no accounts and no tracking, and your text is never sent to a server. The one exception is the mic: dictation uses your browser's speech service, which may send audio to Google or Apple.

Corporate to normal names the bad news first, like layoffs, a hiring freeze, a PIP or a non-answer. Then it goes through the memo sentence by sentence. Normal to corporate keeps every no, question, number, name, date and list, and it won't add meetings or promises you didn't make. It's a joke tool, so read the original before you quit.
